It's not like I'm planning to sail thousands of nautical miles through a mix of
drizzle, rain, and sunshine, but being able to adjust the intermittent wiper
frequency can come in handy from time to time.

The one you need is `17A164LH1` (the original one from the Type R is
`17A164LH2`).
