The Type R didn't have remote central locking, and I felt like doing a bit of
tinkering. And so a plan was born.

I did some reading, and while you can find the central locking control unit
(`H7110AS011`), putting together the entire system: control unit, actuators,
remotes, and wiring harness seems impossible, or at least impossible without
access to a complete donor car. So I settled on something readily available:
Modemix central locking kit. The unit is reasonably well put together: a fuse on
the power supply, a split wire for the indicators, not that bad.

The device offers various options for controlling the actuators. I need the
`+/-` one. This means soldering the orange to the orange-and-black wire, and the
yellow to the yellow-and-black wire. I also removed the green and blue wires
(for closing the windows and opening the boot).

The instruction manual included in the package has been photocopied a billion
times and looks like an artefact discovered by archaeologists at an excavation
site, so I took the liberty of redrawing it myself.

I bought the actuators separately. There are several versions available,
depending on their pulling force: 4 or 8 kg, and their function: master or
slave. A master actuator can send a signal to the other actuators (the slaves)
when the key is turned. My control unit doesn't support master/slave signals,
and since I was set on using this particular fob, I ended up with two 4 kg slave
actuators.

Here's everything installed. The little cube with three screws is the connector
joining the actuator rod to the original door lock linkage; the actuator itself
sits directly underneath it. When connecting the wires from the control unit,
you need to make sure the polarity is correct so that the lock and unlock
buttons on the fob work as intended.

And here's a little surprise: it turns out the Type R did actually have central
locking at some point. On the passenger side (the European passenger side, which
would originally have been the driver's side in Japan), someone had fitted an
aftermarket actuator almost identical to the one I'd just bought. On the
driver’s side (the Japanese passenger side), I found a factory-fitted actuator.
